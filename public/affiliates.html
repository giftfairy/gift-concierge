Affiliate Disclosure

How affiliate links work on Gift Lane.

Gift Lane is free to use. Some of the retailers we recommend are affiliate partners, which helps support the cost of running and improving the service.

1. What is an affiliate link?

Some links on Gift Lane are affiliate links.

If you click one of these links and later make a purchase, the retailer or affiliate network may pay Gift Lane a commission for referring you.

This commission is paid by the retailer and does not normally increase the price you pay.

2. Why we use affiliate partnerships

Affiliate income helps cover the costs of operating Gift Lane, including the technology used to search for products and generate recommendations.

It also allows Gift Lane to remain free for people using Jude to find gift ideas.

3. How affiliate partners affect recommendations

Jude’s curation logic gives approved affiliate partners priority consideration when they have products that are genuinely relevant to the person, occasion, budget and delivery destination.

That does not mean an affiliate product automatically gets recommended.

A non-affiliate product may still be recommended when it is a materially better fit, offers better value, is more appropriate for the recipient, or fills a gap that an affiliate partner cannot.

Put simply: partnerships can influence which strong options get considered first, but suitability still matters.

4. Availability and pricing

Gift Lane does not control retailer pricing, stock levels, shipping costs, delivery times or product availability.

These can change after a recommendation is generated, so you should always check the retailer’s website before purchasing.

5. Third-party tracking

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate network may place a cookie or use similar tracking technology to recognise that Gift Lane referred you.

This allows the retailer or network to attribute an eligible purchase to Gift Lane.

Gift Lane does not receive your card details or retailer account password when you make a purchase.

6. Questions

If you have a question about affiliate links, retailer partnerships or how recommendations work, email concierge@giftlane.au .
