Privacy Policy

How we look after your information.

Gift Lane is designed to be simple to use. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site, why it is used and how it helps us provide the service.

1. Who we are

Gift Lane is an Australian-founded, worldwide gift concierge that helps people find thoughtful gift ideas from real retailers.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at concierge@giftlane.au.

2. Information you provide

When you use Jude, you may enter information such as:

  • who the gift is for
  • their interests, age, stage of life or other details you choose to provide
  • the occasion
  • your budget
  • the country where the gift will be delivered

This information is used to generate personalised gift suggestions.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for finding a gift.

3. AI-powered recommendations

Gift Lane uses artificial intelligence and web-based search tools to help generate gift recommendations.

The information you enter into the gift finder may be processed by service providers used to operate the recommendation system so Jude can return relevant suggestions.

We use the information for the purpose of providing the Gift Lane service. We do not sell the personal information you enter into the gift finder.

4. Analytics

Gift Lane uses Google Analytics to help us understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited, how users arrive at the site and general usage patterns.

Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as browser or device information, approximate location, page visits and interactions.

This information helps us improve Gift Lane and understand which parts of the site are useful to visitors.

5. Affiliate links

Some recommendations on Gift Lane contain affiliate links.

If you click one of these links, the retailer or affiliate network may use cookies or other tracking technology to recognise that Gift Lane referred you.

If you later make a purchase, Gift Lane may receive a commission. This does not normally change the price you pay.

You can read more about this in our Affiliate Disclosure.

6. External retailers and websites

Gift Lane links to third-party retailers and other websites.

Once you leave Gift Lane, the privacy practices of that retailer or website apply. Gift Lane does not control how third-party websites collect or use your information.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation of Gift Lane and limit access to what is needed to provide and maintain the service.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

Gift Lane may update this Privacy Policy as the site, technology or services we use change.

The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Questions

If you have a question about privacy or how information is handled, email concierge@giftlane.au .
